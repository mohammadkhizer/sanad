# Sanad: Content Policy and Moderation

## 1. Principles
- Knowledge with adab (respect): disagreement is allowed, contempt is not.
- Only verified scholars publish. Members engage through comments, questions, and requests.
- Sanad does not adjudicate which tradition is "correct"; it enforces conduct and authenticity.

## 2. Allowed
- Lectures, Q&A, short reels, tafsir/teaching clips, live classes
- Respectful scholarly disagreement with cited reasoning
- Questions from members (comments or approved chat requests)

## 3. Prohibited
| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Hate and takfir | Declaring groups disbelievers, calls for exclusion or violence |
| Violence and extremism | Glorifying or inciting violence, recruitment |
| Harassment | Targeted abuse, doxxing, threats, brigading |
| Impersonation | Claiming scholar status or credentials falsely |
| Misinformation | Fabricated attributions, fake fatwas in a scholar's name |
| Sexual or graphic content | Any explicit or gratuitous material |
| Political campaigning | Party endorsement or electioneering (v1) |
| Spam and scams | Donation fraud, fake giveaways, link farms |

## 4. Enforcement Ladder
1. Content removal plus notice
2. Warning
3. Temporary suspension (7/30 days)
4. Scholar status revoked (badge removed, posting disabled)
5. Permanent ban

Severe cases (violence, threats, impersonation) skip straight to step 3 or higher.

## 5. Reporting and Review
- Any user can report content, a profile, a chat message, or a live stream.
- Reports are triaged: **P0** (threats, violence) within 1h, **P1** within 24h, **P2** within 72h.
- Permanent bans and scholar revocations require a **second moderator or admin approval**.
- Every action is written to the audit log with moderator ID and reason.

## 6. Appeals
- Users can appeal within 14 days in-app.
- Appeals are reviewed by a different moderator than the original decision-maker.

## 7. Live and Calls
- Hosts can mute, remove, and block viewers; moderators can end streams.
- Recording-consent notice is shown before any recorded call or live stream.
- Members need scholar approval before 1:1 chat or calls.

## 8. Scholar Credential Integrity
- Credentials are re-verified periodically (e.g., every 12 months) or on complaint.
- Uploaded credential documents are visible only to moderators and admins, and deleted after the retention window.
